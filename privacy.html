How Google information
is handled.

Updated 14 October 2026

Purpose and responsibility

Clarence Personal is a private application operated by its account holder for personal administration. Questions about the application or its handling of data can be sent to apanzd@gmail.com.

Information accessed

With the account holder’s authorization, the assistant can access Gmail messages, drafts, attachments and settings; Google Drive files and metadata; Google Docs and Sheets content; Calendar events and calendars; and Contacts. Its granted permissions also permit creating, editing, sending or deleting information when carrying out an authorized task.

A separate background crawler maintains the private searchable archive. Its connection retains the same Google permissions as the assistant; its routine synchronization reads Gmail and Drive information. Actions beyond synchronization remain subject to the account holder’s authorization.

Use and processing

Information is used to provide the personal-assistant functions requested by the account holder: finding and summarizing material, preparing drafts, maintaining a private searchable archive and performing authorized actions. Selected information may be processed by AI services and other service providers configured by the account holder to carry out those tasks.

The application does not sell Google account data or use it for advertising. Its use and transfer of information received from Google APIs is governed by the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and access

Working files, task records and archive material may be stored on devices and in private storage controlled by the account holder. OAuth credentials and authorization tokens are stored in macOS Keychain. They are not included in this website or its public source.

Retention, deletion and revocation

Working records and archive material are retained as needed for the account holder’s tasks until the account holder removes them. The account holder can request deletion or remove local working material and private archives directly.

Authorization can be revoked through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access; it does not automatically delete material already retained in private working files or archives.

This website

This static website has no sign-in, Google authorization form, advertising, analytics scripts or tracking pixels. Its hosting provider may process ordinary web-request logs under that provider’s privacy policy. No Google account content is published here.
