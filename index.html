A Google connection for
everyday personal work.

Clarence Personal helps its account holder work with email, files, documents, spreadsheets, calendars and contacts.

It connects to the account holder’s Google account only after Google authorization. The assistant can find and summarize information, prepare drafts and carry out requested administrative tasks using the permissions granted by the account holder.

One account holder. One private workspace.

Clarence Personal is for private, single-user use. It is not a public service and has no public registration. A separate background crawler can keep a private archive of Gmail and Drive information for search.

This website describes the application. It does not connect to Google accounts or display account data.

Control stays with the account holder.

Google permissions can be reviewed and revoked through Google Account connections. The privacy notice explains what information the assistant accesses, how it is used and how to request deletion.
